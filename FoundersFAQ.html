What Is An OAPP?

OAPPs are applications built within the OASIS ecosystem infrastructure.

Founder tiers include expanded monthly OAPP creation allowances which may be used for ecosystem apps, tools, services, experiences, creator systems and integrations depending on platform capabilities and future releases.

What Are GeoNFTs?

GeoNFTs are geographically aware NFTs designed for use within the OASIS ecosystem and future Omniverse experiences.

GeoNFT systems are already operational and will continue expanding with future integrations.

How Do The Founder NFTs Work?

Founder NFTs function as digital Founder access passes within the OASIS ecosystem.

  • Currently they are minted on the Solana blockchain
  • They are stored within the holder's connected Web3 wallet
  • They are also linked to the user’s avatar through a wrapped Web4 NFT integration layer

This hybrid architecture helps bridge traditional Web3 ownership with deeper Web4 ecosystem integrations across OASIS systems.

As the ecosystem expands, Founder NFTs will progressively unlock deeper functionality across avatars, games, AI systems, creator tooling, apps, rewards, digital identity, permissions and Omniverse ecosystem experiences.

What Happens After Purchase?

New users

If you do not already have an OASIS Avatar account:

  1. Complete purchase using crypto or supported card payment methods.
  2. An OASIS Avatar account is automatically created using your purchase email address.
  3. Your Founder NFT is delivered to your connected Web3 wallet.
  4. A wrapped Web4 Founder NFT is linked to your newly created OASIS Avatar identity.
  5. You receive an email containing your account details and a secure activation link.
  6. You create your own password during first login for security.
  7. Your account is ready for OPORTAL and future ecosystem systems as they become available.

Existing users

If an OASIS Avatar already exists for your email address, a new avatar account will not be created. Your Founder NFT will be linked to your existing avatar, your Founder status and Web4 NFT integrations will attach to your current ecosystem identity, and your Web3 NFT will remain within your connected wallet.

Where Are The NFTs Stored?

Founder NFTs currently exist in two connected layers:

Web3 NFT layer

The NFT is stored within the holder's connected blockchain wallet. Current chain support is Solana and current wallet support is Phantom.

Web4 avatar layer

A wrapped Web4 NFT layer links the Founder NFT to the user’s OASIS Avatar identity within the ecosystem.

This enables future integrations across OPORTAL, avatars, games, AI systems, creator systems, digital identity, permissions, rewards and Omniverse ecosystem functionality.
