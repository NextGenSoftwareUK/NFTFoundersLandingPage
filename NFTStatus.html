Why Phantom and Solflare May Not Be Showing Your NFT Yet

Phantom and Solflare use their own indexing and data services to display NFTs. These systems can take time to recognise newly created collections or updated metadata. Your NFT is not missing from the blockchain — it simply has not yet been picked up by these third-party display layers.

This can occur with newly created Solana NFT collections as third-party services build and refresh their indexes. When a collection is newly created or updated, this process can take anywhere from a few hours to several days. This is entirely outside our control.

This is not a sign anything is wrong. The NFT exists on-chain, is verified, and is fully usable in the OASIS ecosystem right now. Phantom and Solflare simply have not caught up yet.

We are actively engaging with Helius, Phantom, and Solflare directly to request prioritised re-indexing of the OASIS Founders Collection. We are also exploring listing on Magic Eden and Tensor, as marketplace listings are known to trigger immediate indexer pickup.

The RugCheck Score — Understanding the Flags

RugCheck is a tool designed primarily to detect fraudulent meme coins and fungible tokens, where a live Mint Authority means the creator can print unlimited supply and dump on holders. It applies the same logic to NFTs, which creates several false positives.

Here is a breakdown of every flag that may appear on your OASIS Founders NFT:

FlagVerdictExplanation
Mint Authority enabled False positive When Metaplex creates the Master Edition (which enforces supply = 1), it transfers the Mint Authority to a Program Derived Address controlled entirely by the Metaplex program — not any wallet. Nobody can mint more of this token. This behaviour is a characteristic of standard Metaplex NFT collections and is not by itself an indicator of risk.
Freeze Authority enabled False positive Same root cause. The Freeze Authority is held by the Metaplex Master Edition PDA, not by any human-controlled wallet. No one can freeze your token account. This applies to every standard Metaplex NFT on Solana.
Mutable metadata Fixed — immutable We have set isMutable = false on all OASIS Founders NFTs. The metadata is permanently frozen on-chain and can never be altered by anyone, including us.

Why We Cannot Revoke Mint & Freeze Authority Yet

RugCheck and some guides suggest revoking Mint and Freeze Authority to achieve a clean score. We fully intend to do this — but it cannot be done until the collection has completely sold out and all 170 NFTs have been minted.

The reason: revoking these authorities while minting is still active would permanently break the ability to mint the remaining NFTs in the collection. Doing so prematurely would harm buyers who have not yet received their NFT. This is entirely standard practice across the Solana NFT ecosystem. Every major project — DeGods, Mad Lads, Okay Bears, and countless others — kept these authorities live during their mint phase and only revoked them at or after sellout. Once our collection is fully minted, we will revoke both authorities immediately and RugCheck's score will reflect that.

The RugCheck warnings on Mint and Freeze Authority are completely outside our control for the duration of the mint. They do not indicate any risk to existing holders — your NFT is on-chain, immutable, and fully secured. Every other Solana NFT project that is mid-mint is in exactly the same position.

We have reported these false positives to RugCheck. The Mint and Freeze Authority flags are a structural characteristic of all Metaplex NFTs during an active mint — they cannot be cleared without destroying the ability to mint the remaining collection. We are continuing to engage with RugCheck to ensure correct classification of NFTs vs fungible tokens.
