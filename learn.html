The Problem

Why OASIS Exists

The internet has become fragmented. Every application has its own identity. Every community lives on a different platform. Every AI operates in isolation. Every organisation rebuilds the same infrastructure. Every innovation creates another silo. OASIS was created to solve this — not by replacing existing technologies, but by enabling them to work together.

Fragmented Identity
Every platform demands its own account. Users rebuild their identity from scratch, repeatedly.
Isolated Communities
Communities live on incompatible networks with no way to connect, collaborate or exchange value.
Disconnected AI
AI systems operate in isolation instead of collaborating. Intelligence is trapped in silos.
Duplicated Infrastructure
Organisations rebuild the same foundational systems over and over, wasting time and resources.
Compounding Complexity
Every new technology creates another layer of disconnected tools, APIs and incompatible standards.
Missed Opportunities
Innovation that could connect creates new silos instead. The potential of the internet goes unrealised.
No Digital Sovereignty
Users don't own their data, identity or digital assets. Platforms can revoke access, change terms or disappear overnight.
Broken User Experience
Switching between disconnected apps means constant friction — re-authentication, data re-entry and lost context at every boundary.

The Stack

Powered by the OASIS Ecosystem

OASIS is not a single platform. It is an interoperable ecosystem of technologies that connect applications, intelligence, identities, communities and digital infrastructure into one unified network.

OASIS API
Web4
The universal interoperability, abstraction and aggregation layer connecting Web1, Web2, Web3 and future technologies through one unified API. Provides universal identity, avatars, reputation (karma), data, assets, provider abstraction and cross-platform interoperability.
STAR API
Web5
The core API powering the Experience Layer, enabling interoperable applications, worlds, quests, missions, achievements, gamification and shared digital experiences across the OASIS ecosystem.
STARNET
Marketplace
The decentralised App, Asset and Intelligence Marketplace where developers can discover, publish, deploy and monetise OAPPs, Smart Bricks, Holons, AI agents, workflows, APIs, plugins, templates, virtual worlds, digital assets and interoperable services.
OGEngine
Omniverse Runtime
The interoperable Omniverse runtime connecting games, virtual worlds and interactive experiences through shared identity, assets, inventory, quests, achievements, AI and persistent progression.
OPORTAL
Gateway
The unified gateway providing seamless access to every application, identity, service, world and experience across the OASIS ecosystem.
ONET
Networking
The resilient networking layer providing secure communication and connectivity across the ecosystem.
Our World
Flagship Experience
The flagship real-world experience that bridges the physical and digital worlds through location-based exploration, sustainability, wellbeing, gamification and interoperable digital experiences.

Each component remains sovereign. Together they become exponentially more powerful because they are intentionally designed to interoperate.

The Roadmap

The OASIS Stack

From universal interoperability to the convergence of all things — a layered architecture for the evolution of a connected world.

W4
The Unification Layer
Web4
The universal interoperability, abstraction and aggregation layer connecting Web1, Web2, Web3 and future technologies through one unified API. Web4 provides universal identity, avatars, reputation (karma), assets, data and provider abstraction — forming the foundation for the entire OASIS ecosystem.
W5
The Experience Layer
Web5
Transforms the unified infrastructure into living digital experiences. Web5 powers the Omniverse through interoperable applications (OAPPs), STARNET, OGEngine, shared worlds, cross-world assets, quests, achievements and immersive experiences built on top of Web4.
W6
The Intelligence Layer
Web6
The interoperability, abstraction and aggregation layer for artificial intelligence and future forms of intelligence. Web6 enables intelligent systems to discover, orchestrate, communicate, reason and collaborate across multiple AI providers through one unified intelligence network.
W7
The Symbiotic Layer
Web7
The conscious human-AI symbiosis layer. Designed for non-invasive, organic collaboration where human intuition, creativity and wisdom work in harmony with machine intelligence. Human and AI become partners — not replacements.
W8
The Inter-Galactic Layer
Web8
Earth becomes one node within a wider connected mesh of worlds and civilisations. A fractal, holonic architecture designed to extend interoperability beyond our planet into future interplanetary and inter-galactic digital ecosystems.
W9
The Singularity Layer
Web9
The Great Convergence. The layer where the distinctions between physical and digital, biological and artificial, human and galactic dissolve into one unified network. Intelligence recognises itself as part of a single interconnected whole.
W10
The Source
Web10
The origin and destination of the entire stack. Pure undifferentiated consciousness — the Alpha and the Omega. Web10 completes the circle, where all layers ultimately emerge from and return to the same Source.
