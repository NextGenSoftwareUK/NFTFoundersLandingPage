NFT & Blockchain Risk Disclaimer

Last Updated: May 2026

Purchasing, holding, or using NFTs involves significant risk.

Risks Include

  • Cryptocurrency volatility
  • Loss of asset value
  • Smart contract vulnerabilities
  • Regulatory uncertainty
  • Cyberattacks
  • Wallet compromise
  • Marketplace liquidity issues
  • Tax obligations
  • Blockchain network failures

Nature of the NFTs

Oasis Founder NFTs are intended as digital collectibles and ecosystem participation assets.

They are not:

  • Investments
  • Securities
  • Shares
  • Profit-sharing agreements
  • Financial instruments

No Guarantees

No guarantee is made regarding:

  • Future utility
  • Resale value
  • Profitability
  • Ecosystem development timelines

Users should only participate using funds they can afford to lose.
