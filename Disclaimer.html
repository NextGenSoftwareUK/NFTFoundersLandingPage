NFT & Blockchain Risk Disclaimer

Purchasing, holding or using NFTs involves significant risk. This page summarizes the main risks in a clear, plain-language format.

Risks Include

  • Cryptocurrency volatility
  • Loss of asset value
  • Smart contract vulnerabilities
  • Regulatory uncertainty
  • Cyberattacks
  • Wallet compromise
  • Marketplace liquidity issues
  • Tax obligations
  • Blockchain network failures

Nature of the NFTs

Oasis Founder NFTs are intended as digital collectibles and ecosystem participation assets.

They are not investments, securities, shares, profit-sharing agreements or financial instruments.

No Guarantees

No guarantee is made regarding future utility, resale value, profitability, ecosystem development timelines or market performance.

Users should only participate using funds they can afford to lose.
